None of it happened — it's an alternate timeline. But almost none of it is the AI's idea either. Nearly every step was asked for in public at the time, by lawyers, village councils, opposition MPs, newspapers, a retired ship pilot. The advice was given. It just wasn't taken.

Same method as before, Claude on Opus 5, now over 295 published documents.

19 JUL — Blame nobody on day one. No "mischief". Say instead: she was licensed for 397 people and carried 250 life jackets, that doesn't add up, and I don't know why yet. Both numbers were public that day. Nobody has explained them since.

20 JUL — Publish the rescue log, gaps and all. And say what the wharf video shows about the CARGO, not just the headcount. The state has had that video since day two and has never said a word about the cargo — the thing three men were charged over.

21 JUL — One set of numbers daily, including the total who never came home. The Government has never published that total. Newspapers had to work it out by subtraction.

22 JUL — MARAD steps back. It passed this ship as fit to sail. It can't be the one to say the ship was fit.

23 JUL — Two ministers step aside. Not resign. Step aside, so they're not running the department the inquiry is examining. The Transparency Institute asked for exactly that, naming both, on exactly that day.

23 JUL — Ask about the deadline. Families may have only until about 18 January 2027 to start a claim. In fourteen days nobody in Government has said the word "limitation" once. That one isn't history. That's a clock, and it's running.

25 JUL — Nothing goes to grieving families about the wreck until the divers report. In real life the memorial paper went out on the 25th. The divers said too dangerous on the 27th.

28 JUL — Settle how the inquiry takes evidence before anyone is charged. Four ways of doing it were published. The inquiry was sworn in with no rules at all. Nobody chose.
