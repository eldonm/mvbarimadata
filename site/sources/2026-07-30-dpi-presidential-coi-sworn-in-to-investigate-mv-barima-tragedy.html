Presidential CoI sworn in to investigate MV Barima tragedy

What this source establishes

This is the first OFFICIAL statement of the Commission's statutory basis: "The CoI was established under Section 2(1) of the Commissions of Inquiry Act, Chapter 19:03, to investigate the circumstances surrounding the capsizing and sinking of the MV Barima, owned and operated by the Transport and Harbours Department, on the night of July 18, 2026." The oath was administered by Chief Magistrate Judy Latchman at State House, Georgetown; Justice Smith participated virtually.

Key claims

  • This is the first OFFICIAL statement of the Commission's statutory basis: "The CoI was established under Section 2(1) of the Commissions of Inquiry Act, Chapter 19:03, to investigate the circumstances surrounding the capsizing and sinking of the MV Barima, owned and operated by the Transport and Harbours Department, on the night of July 18, 2026."
  • The oath was administered by Chief Magistrate Judy Latchman at State House, Georgetown; Justice Smith participated virtually.
  • "The commission is expected to submit its findings, observations and recommendations to President Ali upon completion of its work." No reporting deadline is stated.
  • Ali: "The families who have suffered unimaginable loss await these answers. Future generations deserve the assurance that every lesson that can be learned from this tragedy will indeed be learned."
  • Government will provide "all administrative, technical and financial and logistical support required" — no figure, no vote, no appropriation cited.
  • CONTRADICTS ARCHIVE: the official release states "72 bodies were recovered, 30 could not be accounted for, and 76 were rescued" out of an estimated 179 aboard. Every official figure in the archive since 24 July gave 73 recovered.
  • No gazette reference, no secretary, no call for submissions, no budget appears in the official release.
