Guyanese press news 24 July 2026

Govt introduces interim cargo service to ensure essential supplies reach Port Kaituma

iNews Guyana · INEWS (staff, no individual byline)

Read the original at iNews Guyana ↗

What this source establishes

iNews Guyana reports that the Ministry of Public Works announced an interim cargo arrangement to keep essential supplies flowing to Port Kaituma while transport services remain disrupted after the MV Barima sinking. An open barge will carry cargo and essential supplies only to the Region One community, with no passengers accommodated. Loading begins Saturday 25 July at the Kingston Goods Wharf, with departure from Georgetown set for Monday 27 July at 5:00 p.m., and the ministry said weather-sensitive items must be securely packaged because the open barge exposes cargo to the elements. The service is free of charge and is intended to sustain supplies until regular shipping resumes.

Key claims

  • The Ministry of Public Works announced an interim cargo arrangement to ensure continued delivery of cargo and essential supplies to Port Kaituma while normal transport services remain affected.
  • An open barge will be used exclusively to transport cargo and essential supplies to the Region One community; no passengers will be accommodated.
  • Loading of cargo will commence on Saturday, July 25, at the Kingston Goods Wharf.
  • The barge is scheduled to depart Georgetown for Port Kaituma on Monday, July 27 at 5:00 p.m.
  • The ministry urged businesses, suppliers and residents to securely package all weather-sensitive items because the open barge exposes cargo to the elements during the voyage.
  • The temporary service is being provided free of charge.
  • Contact numbers for additional information are 658-2050 and 501-0561.
  • The interim arrangement is intended to support residents until regular shipping operations resume.

Actors named

  • Ministry of Public Works, Guyana government ministry operating the interim cargo arrangement
  • Port Kaituma, Region One community served by the barge
  • Kingston Goods Wharf, Georgetown loading point
