Key claims

  • Gives the MV Ma Lisha as length overall 69.55 m, beam 13.5 m, draught 3.0 m, displacement 1,700 tonnes
  • Capacity 276 passengers and 18 crew; 250 tonnes cargo comprising 14 cars, 2 lorries and 10 containers
  • Freight is handled by 'a forward vehicle ramp'; 'passengers board and disembark via side gangways'
  • ABS class, 15 knots, 500 nautical mile range, described as ocean-going; US$12.7M from Garden Reach Shipbuilders, India
  • The forward vehicle ramp establishes that the Ma Lisha is a roll-on/roll-off vessel requiring a ramp to discharge vehicles
  • [CONTESTED] Capacity conflicts with Kaieteur News, which gives 250 passengers, 12 sedans and 2 trucks
