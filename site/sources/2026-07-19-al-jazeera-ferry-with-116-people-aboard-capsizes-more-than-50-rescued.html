International press wire 19 July 2026

Guyana ferry with 116 people on board capsizes; more than 50 rescued

Al Jazeera · AFP

Read the original at Al Jazeera ↗

What this source establishes

Al Jazeera's first-day AFP-sourced report, carrying the lowest counts in this corpus at 116 aboard and 53 rescued. It locates the capsizing near Iron Punt rather than the Pomeroon River, a geographic variant that recurs across the corpus. Edghill's safety-equipment inventory here cites eight lifeboats, differing from the two rigid plus six inflatable rafts other outlets report. No deaths are confirmed at the time of publication.

Figures cited in this source

116 passengers and crew aboard; 53 rescued; dozens missing; no deaths yet confirmed

Key claims

  • MV Barima issued a distress call at about 11pm Saturday (03:00 GMT Sunday) and capsized near Iron Punt en route Georgetown to Port Kaituma — local authorities
  • 53 people had been rescued, with more expected as daylight search progresses — Prime Minister Mark Phillips, to AFP
  • Search involves Guyana's coastguard and private vessels — Public Works Minister Juan Edghill
  • Ferry was equipped with some 250 life jackets, eight lifeboats and other safety gear — Juan Edghill
  • A medical team was being dispatched to treat people 'as soon as they are rescued' — Juan Edghill
  • Circumstances surrounding the accident were not immediately clear

Quotations

  • “There is an active search and rescue operation that is taking place, and we pray for the safety of all”— Juan Edghill, Public Works Minister, in a Facebook video post
