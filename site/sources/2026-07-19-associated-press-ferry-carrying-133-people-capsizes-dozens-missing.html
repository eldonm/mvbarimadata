International press wire 19 July 2026

A ferry carrying 133 people capsizes off Guyana's coast, leaving dozens missing

Associated Press (via Citrus County Chronicle / chronicleonline.com) · Bert Wilkinson - Associated Press

Read the original at Associated Press (via Citrus County Chronicle / chronicleonline.com) ↗

What this source establishes

The AP dispatch by Bert Wilkinson, filed from Georgetown the day after the sinking. It reports 133 passengers and crew aboard and 67 rescued, revising an earlier AP version preserved in the URL slug that said 116 aboard and 53 rescued. Prime Minister Phillips gives a demographic breakdown of survivors and confirms a 1,000-square-mile search zone. Minister Edghill denies overloading and attributes the capsizing to the tide.

Figures cited in this source

133 passengers and crew aboard; 67 rescued (41 men, 11 women, 15 children); dozens missing; no confirmed dead

Key claims

  • A government ferry carrying 133 passengers and crew capsized with 67 people rescued so far — authorities, Sunday
  • The rescued group included 41 men, 11 women and 15 children — Prime Minister Mark Phillips, who is leading the government's response
  • Helicopters, planes and fishing vessels are scanning a 1,000-square-mile area
  • The MV Barima departed shortly after 3 p.m. Saturday and ran into trouble shortly before midnight — officials
  • A distress call was received at 11:01 p.m. (0301 GMT) — Public Works Minister Juan Edghill, on Facebook
  • The vessel makes weekly trips to the largely Indigenous communities near Venezuela and capsized near the Pomeroon River — authorities
  • The vessel was fitted with 250 life jackets, two rigid life rafts and six inflatable life rafts — Juan Edghill
  • Edghill denied claims it could have capsized because it was overloaded with passengers or cargo
  • 'It had to do with the tide' — Juan Edghill, referencing statements from rescued passengers

Quotations

  • “It had to do with the tide”— Juan Edghill, Public Works Minister, referencing statements from rescued passengers
