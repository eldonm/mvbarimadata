Guyanese press news 21 July 2026

MV Barima Tragedy: 53 bodies now recovered; total number of rescued persons revised to 76

News Room Guyana

Read the original at News Room Guyana ↗

What this source establishes

News Room Guyana gives the clearest domestic explanation of why the survivor count moved backwards, from 77 to 76: information arriving from multiple uncoordinated sources was reviewed and de-duplicated. It documents the morgue logistics, with four refrigerated containers taken to Suddie Hospital, and puts the search area at 2,100 square kilometres. It is the first News Room report to state that the captain and first engineer were arrested after testing positive for narcotics, and that the passenger list was released publicly only after the manifest discrepancies came to light.

Figures cited in this source

53 bodies recovered, 26 positively identified; rescued revised down to 76; 116 passengers + 17 crew on manifest

Key claims

  • Prime Minister Mark Phillips said at a Tuesday afternoon press conference that 53 bodies had been recovered, 26 positively identified.
  • Bodies are being stored at Suddie Hospital on the Essequibo Coast; four refrigerated containers were taken to the hospital for storage.
  • Another body has been identified inside the sunken vessel but divers have not yet been able to recover it; the PM believes there may be other bodies inside.
  • The number of rescued persons was revised to 76, with the adjustment resulting from de-duplication of information received from several different sources.
  • The search area was expanded to 2,100 square kilometres.
  • Only 35 of the 67 rescued were listed on the passenger manifest, meaning 32 survivors were never officially recorded.
  • The cargo manifest showed 268 tonnes of cargo, below the licensed capacity of 284 tonnes, but that figure is now also under question.
  • The vessel's captain and first engineer were arrested after both tested positive for narcotics.
  • President Irfaan Ali promised a full and thorough investigation.
