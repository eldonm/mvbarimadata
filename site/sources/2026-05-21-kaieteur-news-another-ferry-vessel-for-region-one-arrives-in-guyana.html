What this source establishes

A Kaieteur News report two months before the sinking recording that the Kalliopi N, a 2024-model Greek-built roll-on/roll-off ferry 50 metres long, bought for the Georgetown-Region One route, sat at Kingston Goods Wharf awaiting commissioning while the 1939 MV Barima continued working the route. It gives capacity as 264 passengers, 82 cars and 19 trucks, and records a tour by Minister Edghill with T&HD General Manager Yolanda Hughes and MARAD Director General Captain Stephen Thomas. Commissioning was tied to three new wharves at New Kingston, Mabaruma and Manawarin, and the report notes 'the president himself will do the commissioning at the appropriate time'.

Key claims

  • Kalliopi N: roll-on/roll-off, 2024 model, Greek-built, 50 metres long
  • Full air conditioning; 264 passengers, 82 cars, 19 trucks
  • Toured at Kingston Goods Wharf by Minister Edghill with T&HD GM Yolanda Hughes and MARAD DG Captain Stephen Thomas
  • Commissioning tied to three new wharves: New Kingston Goods Wharf, Mabaruma Wharf, Manawarin Wharf
  • Region One would have four vessels in total
