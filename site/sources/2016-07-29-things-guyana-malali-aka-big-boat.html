What this source establishes

A Things Guyana heritage post on the MV Malali, recording that she was built in Guyana by Sprostons Ltd with her keel laid on 31 May 1961, to the drawings of the MV Torani and MV Makouria but modified for a live-aboard crew. It states she has served on all three major rivers, the Essequibo, Demerara and Berbice, and that in 2017 the Ministry of Public Works tendered docking and rehabilitation work estimated at G$85-100 million. The post gives no tonnage, dimensions or passenger capacity.
