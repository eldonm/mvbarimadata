Guyanese press news 25 July 2026

APNU submits parliamentary motion to discuss scope of MV Barima inquiry

Kaieteur News

Read the original at Kaieteur News ↗

What this source establishes

Kaieteur News reports that APNU Lead MP Dr Terrence Campbell wrote to Speaker Manzoor Nadir on Friday seeking an adjournment of the National Assembly to debate the Terms of Reference for a Commission of Inquiry into the sinking. Campbell called the request urgent and of public importance, citing significant loss of life and acute public concern, and listed concerns over vessel seaworthiness, regulatory compliance, oversight by maritime authorities, the search and rescue response and prevention of recurrence. APNU described the disaster as the worst maritime disaster in Guyana's recent history with a death toll already surpassing 70, and tabled a proposed ToR with eight areas of focus.

Key claims

  • APNU Lead MP Dr. Terrence Campbell on Friday submitted a correspondence to Speaker of the National Assembly Manzoor Nadir ahead of Monday's Sitting, requesting an adjournment of the Assembly to discuss the Terms of Reference for a Commission of Inquiry into the sinking of the MV Barima.
  • The 87-year-old vessel capsized off seven miles offshore, near Iron Pun in the vicinity of the Pomeroon River, with almost 200 passengers and crew members, along with over 200 tonnes of cargo on Saturday night.
  • The vessel was heading to Port Kaituma, Region One.
  • APNU described the tragedy of 18th July 2026 as 'the worst maritime disaster in Guyana's recent history' with a death toll already surpassing 70 persons.
  • APNU stated the immediate need to establish a fully independent CoI with clear terms of reference to probe the circumstances, causes and consequences of the incident.
  • Dr. Campbell qualified the grounds for his request as 'urgent and of public importance,' citing substantial damage to property and significant loss of lives causing acute public concern and distress.
  • The MP stressed that concerns abound regarding the sea worthiness of vessels, regulatory compliance, oversight by maritime authorities, search and rescue response, the availability of other vessels and measures to prevent recurrence.
  • The Opposition submitted a proposed ToR for debate and adoption identifying eight key areas of focus: establishing facts surrounding the incident; determining cause and contributing factors; examining search-and-rescue response adequacy; assessing regulatory framework and oversight practices; examining allegations of negligence or misconduct; recommending safety improvements; requiring documents and witnesses; and adopting interim protective measures.
  • Dr. Campbell suggested the Assembly also discuss a specific timeframe, membership criteria including independence and expertise, and budgetary arrangements for the commission.

Quotations

  • “the worst maritime disaster in Guyana's recent history”— A Partnership for National Unity (APNU), parliamentary opposition party
  • “urgent and of public importance”— Dr. Terrence Campbell, Lead Member of Parliament for APNU, in his letter to the Speaker
  • “An immediate, transparent, parliamentary discussion of proposed terms of reference will assist in ensuring public confidence, facilitate timely establishment of an independent commission and allow the Assembly to give direction on scope, independence and resources.”— Dr. Terrence Campbell, Lead Member of Parliament for APNU
