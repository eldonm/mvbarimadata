What this source establishes

A tertiary fleet and route directory, archived for one structural fact stated nowhere else in this corpus as plainly: the Georgetown-Region One service has no fixed timetable, with each sailing 'announced individually, typically about a week ahead', unlike the daily Parika services. It lists the MV Barima as out of service on that route, gives fares as heavily subsidised at G$1.5 billion in Budget 2026, and records the MV Konawaruk 1899 active from December 2025, the Kalliopi N (284 passengers, 51 vehicles) awaiting commissioning, and the MV Ma Lisha (294 passengers, 250t cargo) active since 2023.
