Guyanese press news 21 July 2026

National Toshaos Council Conference Postponed To A Day Later Amid Mv Barima Tragedy – Location Of M.V Barima Conformed

HGPTV Nightly News · Hgp Nightly News Staff; in-body byline: Antonio Dey, HGP Nightly News reporter

Read the original at HGPTV Nightly News ↗

What this source establishes

HGP TV reports that the ceremonial opening of the 2026 National Toshaos Council Conference at Liliendaal was postponed by a day as Amerindian leaders and village captains gathered in grief over the MV Barima sinking. Vice President Bharrat Jagdeo, at an impromptu consultation on Monday afternoon, said 10 bodies had officially been recovered and that divers had pinpointed the submerged vessel's coordinates. The report lists government measures: an emergency hotline for families reporting missing relatives, cross-referencing of submissions against hospital admission logs and field rescue manifests, presidential site visits across Regions One and Two, and continued police custody for crew members who tested positive for cannabis traces.

Key claims

  • The official opening of the 2026 National Toshaos Council (NTC) Conference was formally postponed on Monday as hundreds of Amerindian leaders, village captains and state officials gathered at the Arthur Chung Conference Centre in grief following the MV Barima disaster.
  • Indigenous leaders from Region One (Barima-Waini) — the primary destination of the ill-fated vessel — struggled to come to terms with loss of life affecting their home communities.
  • Vice President Dr Bharrat Jagdeo confirmed at an impromptu open consultation session on Monday afternoon that 10 bodies had officially been recovered by joint rescue teams.
  • Search-and-rescue divers have pinpointed the exact underwater coordinates of the submerged vessel.
  • A dedicated emergency hotline has been established for families to report missing relatives, with authorities cross-referencing public submissions against hospital admission logs and field rescue manifests to build an accurate roster of those on board.
  • President Dr Irfaan Ali spent Monday conducting site visits across operational zones in Region One (Barima-Waini) and Region Two (Pomeroon-Supenaam), meeting privately with grieving families and monitoring Coast Guard staging areas.
  • Jagdeo reiterated that surviving crew members who tested positive for traces of cannabis remain in police custody as criminal investigators probe the circumstances of the ferry's departure and dispatch.
  • Conference organisers confirmed the formal opening of the NTC Conference would take place on Tuesday, July 21, 2026, under the theme 'Empowering Leadership while Building Sustainable Village Economies through inclusive partnership', with President Ali to deliver the feature address.

Quotations

  • “We are still hopeful, and we are not discontinuing our efforts”— Dr Bharrat Jagdeo, Vice President of Guyana
  • “We are pushing every available resource on the water to ensure we find anyone who might still be out there”— Dr Bharrat Jagdeo, Vice President of Guyana
