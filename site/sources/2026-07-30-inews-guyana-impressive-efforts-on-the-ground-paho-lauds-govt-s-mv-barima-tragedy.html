Key claims

  • FIRST INTERNATIONAL-ORGANISATION COMMENT ON THE RESPONSE in the corpus: PAHO Country Representative in Guyana Dr Kim Eva Dickson praises the state response after a visit to Region One (Barima-Waini).
  • Dickson: "We were really impressed with the efforts on the ground… The Ministry of Health is doing so much on the ground in Region One."
  • The remarks were made "on Wednesday during the signing of an agreement between the Health Ministry and PAHO/WHO" — i.e. at an unrelated One Health signing, not at a Barima event. The praise is therefore incidental, not the product of any assessment mission into the disaster.
  • "The Government has already covered the funeral expenses for dozens of victims of the ferry accident."
  • "Since a majority of the victims as well as survivors hail from that Barima region, the Government has established help centres for grieving families of those still missing. Medical and counselling services are also being offered to both survivors and other impacting relatives."
  • Toll given as "76 persons being rescued, 73 bodies recovered and 30 others still unaccounted for."
  • NO IMO INVOLVEMENT IS REPORTED. PAHO's comment is public-health, not maritime; nothing in this survey shows the International Maritime Organization engaged.
