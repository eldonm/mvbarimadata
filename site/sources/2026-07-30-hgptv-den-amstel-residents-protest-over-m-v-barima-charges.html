Key claims

  • Confirms the 3 August Charity Magistrate's Court date is UNCHANGED as at 30 July: "The three accused remain on remand pending their next court appearance on August 3 at the Charity Magistrate's Court." No bail application, no further charge, no variation is reported anywhere in this survey.
  • The Den Amstel demonstration took place "Wednesday" (29 July) and "centered on defending Goods Superintendent Delon Granderson, Captain Kevin Price, and Chief Mate Rondell Dwayne Roberts, who were remanded in custody after appearing before Chief Magistrate Judy Latchman at Georgetown Magistrates' Court."
  • "Demonstrators carried placards declaring 'Jail the criminals, free the innocent!' and argued the capital charges represented premature prosecution."
  • "Protesters contended that systemic failures—including the vessel's structural condition and loading procedures—fell under ministerial responsibility rather than individual crew accountability." Recorded as protesters' contention, not as finding.
  • "Residents demanded the resignations of Minister of Public Works Bishop Juan Edghill and Minister Deodat Indar, while calling for a transparent Commission of Inquiry process led by Justice Godfrey Smith before any prosecutions proceed."
  • Simultaneous protests are confirmed "at Melanie Damishana on East Coast Demerara and outside the Office of the President in Georgetown."
  • HGPTV gives the toll as "the July 18 capsizing of the M.V. Barima that killed 72 people."
