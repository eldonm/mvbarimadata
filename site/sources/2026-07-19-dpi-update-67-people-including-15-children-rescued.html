Official document operational update 19 July 2026

Update: 67 people, including 15 children rescued from MV Barima

Department of Public Information

Read the original at Department of Public Information ↗

What this source establishes

A short first-day DPI bulletin fixing the rescue count at 67 including 15 children, a figure that never subsequently rises above 77 in this corpus. It quotes the Prime Minister committing to continue the operation 'until every effort has been exhausted', a formula repeated with slight variations through the following week. It also lists the three information centres relied on for family notification.

Official statement recorded in this source

"The operation will continue until every effort has been exhausted." — PM Mark Phillips

Figures cited in this source

67 people rescued, including 15 children

Key claims

  • 67 people, including 15 children, rescued as recovery teams advanced their search.
  • PM Phillips: "the operation will continue until every effort has been exhausted."
  • Information centres at Umana Yana Georgetown, Port Kaituma and Kumaka/Mabaruma remain operational to provide verified updates to relatives.

About this archive

An independent, citation-first record of the sinking of the MV Barima on the night of Saturday 18 July 2026, compiled from 206 published documents and checked line by line against them. It is not affiliated with the Government of Guyana, the Transport & Harbours Department, any political party, or any news organisation.

Compiled Sunday 26 July 2026, last revised Monday 27 July 2026. The Commission of Inquiry’s members were named on 26 July 2026, its establishing instrument was not yet gazetted, and figures were still moving. Read Method & gaps before citing anything here.

This site records what the sources say and who said it. It does not advance an explanation of the cause, and it makes no recommendation.
