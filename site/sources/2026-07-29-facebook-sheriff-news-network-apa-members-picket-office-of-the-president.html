What this source establishes

A social media post by a Guyanese news account reporting that members and supporters of the Amerindian Peoples Association staged a peaceful picket outside the Office of the President on 29 July, demanding accountability for the disaster and the removal of Ministers Edghill and Indar. When filed this was the only record of the picket held here and the archive noted that no press report of it appeared in the corpus. That note was withdrawn on 30 July: Kaieteur News, News Room, Kiskadee Watch and Demerara Waves all carry the picket. The post is retained as the earliest record of it held here.

Key claims

  • [SOCIAL MEDIA] Reports that members and supporters of the Amerindian Peoples Association staged a peaceful picket outside the Office of the President on Wednesday 29 July 2026, demanding accountability for the MV Barima tragedy
  • States that protesters are calling for the removal of Minister of Public Works Juan Edghill and Minister of Public Utilities and Aviation Deodat Indar
  • The post carries a live video of the picket. Placards visible in the video name Edghill and Indar

That is no longer so: Kaieteur News, News Room, Kiskadee Watch and Demerara Waves all carry the picket, and were retrieved on 30 July. The gap note is withdrawn; the post is retained as the earliest record of the event held here.

About this archive
