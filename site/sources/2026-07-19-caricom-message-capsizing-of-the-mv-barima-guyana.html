MESSAGE | Capsizing of the MV Barima, Guyana

Caribbean Community (CARICOM) Secretariat · Caribbean Community (CARICOM)

Read the original at Caribbean Community (CARICOM) Secretariat ↗

What this source establishes

CARICOM's same-day message of solidarity, and the only regional-institutional document in this manifest to fix a geographic location independently of Guyanese ministries: 'off the Atlantic coast near the Pomeroon River'. It takes no position on cause or culpability, confining itself to condolence and tribute, and credits private vessel operators alongside government arms for mobilising overnight. It characterises the event as an 'unfolding disaster' while Guyana was still describing a search and rescue.

Official statement recorded in this source

"CARICOM stands in solidarity with the Government and people of Guyana, in particular, the families and communities that are being impacted by this unfolding disaster."

Key claims

  • CARICOM is "deeply saddened by the capsizing of the passenger ferry, MV Barima, off the Atlantic coast near the Pomeroon River."
  • CARICOM locates the capsizing off the Atlantic coast near the Pomeroon River.
  • CARICOM pays tribute to rescue teams "led by relevant arms of the Government of Guyana, and including private vessel operators, who mobilized overnight in challenging conditions to save lives."
  • CARICOM describes the event as an "unfolding disaster."
