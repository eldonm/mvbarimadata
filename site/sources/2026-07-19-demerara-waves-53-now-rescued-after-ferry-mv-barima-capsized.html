Guyanese press news 19 July 2026

53 now rescued after ferry MV Barima capsized off Essequibo Coast

Demerara Waves · Denis Chabrol

Read the original at Demerara Waves ↗

What this source establishes

A short 5:38 AM Demerara Waves update in which the Prime Minister raises the rescue count from eight to 53 within roughly two hours. No other source in this corpus records that the vessel's position was fixed from an electronic locater transmitter (ELT) signal in addition to the flares. Demerara Waves geolocates the sinking itself using Google Earth Pro at almost 10 nautical miles off the Essequibo Coast, more than the seven miles other outlets reported the same day. This piece gives the vessel's age as 89 years, one of at least four different ages assigned to the ship across the domestic press.

Figures cited in this source

53 rescued (passengers and crew); 116 passengers plus crew aboard

Key claims

  • Prime Minister Mark Phillips said 53 persons had been rescued after the MV Barima capsized off the Essequibo Coast en route to Port Kaituma.
  • Demerara Waves describes the MV Barima as 89 years old.
  • The 53 rescued comprised both passengers and crew, according to the Prime Minister.
  • At the time of the incident the minister said 116 passengers plus crew were aboard.
  • The vessel's location was identified based on an electronic locater transmitter (ELT) signal and emergency flares.
  • Demerara Waves calculated, using Google Earth Pro, that the incident occurred at sea almost 10 nautical miles off the Essequibo Coast.
