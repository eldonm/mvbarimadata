International press news 23 July 2026

Guyana Ferry Disaster: Families Demand Answers as Around 100 Feared Dead

Al Jazeera · Al Jazeera Staff and AP

Read the original at Al Jazeera ↗

What this source establishes

Al Jazeera's write-through of the AP accountability story, published a day later and holding the bodies-recovered figure at 53 where AP had 65. It consolidates the survivor allegations of overloading, mid-voyage engine repair and dismissed water-ingress warnings, and sets them against the government's position that the vessel was under capacity. The Maritime Administration Department head is cited on the need to establish whether supervisory staff acted properly. It retains the Samantha Ramin testimony and the Indian replacement-ferry detail.

Figures cited in this source

Around 100 feared dead; 53 bodies recovered; 76 of roughly 179 aboard survived; manifest listed 133

Key claims

  • Expected death toll has climbed to around 100 — Al Jazeera/AP
  • 76 of roughly 179 people believed aboard survived; 53 bodies recovered — officials, Tuesday
  • 'I know my baby is in that boat' — Samantha Ramin, mother who lost two of her three children
  • The 87-year-old vessel began taking on water and capsized off the Essequibo coast
  • Passengers alleged the ferry was dangerously overloaded with people and heavy cargo including engines, vehicles, fuel containers and commercial goods
  • Survivors allege the ferry suffered engine trouble shortly after departure but the crew attempted repairs at sea rather than turning back, and that concerns about water entering the vessel were dismissed as routine
  • Government officials say the ferry was under its capacity limits for both passengers and cargo but acknowledge conflicting accounts
  • A full investigation is needed to establish how the vessel was loaded and whether supervisory staff acted properly — head of the Maritime Administration Department
  • The government has nothing to hide and anyone found responsible will face legal consequences — Prime Minister Mark Phillips
  • A US$12.7 million replacement ferry purchased from India was awaiting completion of a special pier at Port Kaituma — officials
  • MV Barima last underwent major repairs in 2024 with additional maintenance scheduled for October

Quotations

  • “I know my baby is in that boat”— Samantha Ramin, mother who lost two of her three children
  • “I know he is down there. I know he is down there. I want to see them find my baby”— Samantha Ramin
