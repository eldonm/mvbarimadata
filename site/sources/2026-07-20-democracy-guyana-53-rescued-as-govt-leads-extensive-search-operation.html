Guyanese press news 20 July 2026

53 rescued as gov't leads extensive search operation following MV Barima capsizing

DemocracyGuyana · Reporter Georgetown Guyana

Read the original at DemocracyGuyana ↗

What this source establishes

A DemocracyGuyana report built almost entirely from the Umana Yana press briefing, presenting the state response as a coordinated whole-of-cabinet mobilisation. It is the fullest record in this corpus of who did what inside government: Health Minister Frank Anthony leading medical operations in Regions Two and Three, Deodat Indar joining the search at sea, and Madanlall Ramraj coordinating Essequibo Coast vessels. It preserves the three family information centres and their phone numbers, and records that T&HD personnel staffed those centres using the vessel's manifest, later found to be missing roughly half the survivors.

Figures cited in this source

53 rescued confirmed; 116 passengers and 17 crew aboard

Key claims

  • The MV Barima capsized off the coast of Region Two late Saturday while travelling to Port Kaituma, Region One, with 116 passengers and 17 crew members on board.
  • Prime Minister Brigadier (Ret'd) Mark Phillips said at a press briefing at the Umana Yana on Sunday morning that 53 passengers had been confirmed rescued.
  • Minister of Public Works Bishop Juan Edghill said the National Search and Rescue Coordination Centre was activated shortly after 11:00 p.m. on Saturday.
  • Edghill said every available Coast Guard and MARAD vessel plus private operators from the Essequibo Coast and Region Three joined the rescue mission.
  • Minister of Health Dr Frank Anthony is leading medical operations in Regions Two and Three; Minister Deodat Indar joined the search at sea; Minister Madanlall Ramraj coordinated vessels along the Essequibo Coast.
  • The government established three information centres: Umana Yana Georgetown (658-2050), Port Kaituma Community Centre (662-3577), and NAREI Extension Centre Kumaka (769-3541).
  • Transport and Harbours Department personnel were stationed at the centres to provide verified updates to relatives using the vessel's manifest.
  • Minister within the Office of the Prime Minister Kwame McCoy urged the public to rely only on official updates.

Quotations

  • “As at this time, we've rescued, confirmed the rescue of 53 passengers from the ferry”— Mark Phillips, Prime Minister
  • “The search not only is expanded but is ongoing as I speak. Our effort is aimed at rescuing everybody who was on board that ferry”— Mark Phillips, Prime Minister
  • “All the stakeholders were engaged, and there has been a massive response”— Juan Edghill, Minister of Public Works
  • “We could only share verified information with you at this time”— Kwame McCoy, Minister within the Office of the Prime Minister
