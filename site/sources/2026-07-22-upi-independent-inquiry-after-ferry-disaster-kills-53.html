International press wire 22 July 2026

Guyana to launch independent inquiry after ferry disaster kills 53

UPI · Mar Puig

Read the original at UPI ↗

What this source establishes

UPI's inquiry-focused dispatch, the clearest single record in this corpus of the commission of inquiry's announced remit. No other source in this corpus reports that seabed mapping technology provided with ExxonMobil support located the wreck lying on its starboard side. President Ali is quoted telling relatives rescue had not been ruled out four days after the sinking. It confirms maritime personnel were placed on administrative leave and describes the sinking as the worst in Guyana's history rather than in decades.

Figures cited in this source

At least 53 dead; 76 rescued; about 179 aboard; manifest listed 116 passengers and 17 crew; dozens unaccounted for

Key claims

  • An independent commission of inquiry will be established 'as early as possible' — Prime Minister Mark Phillips, Wednesday
  • 'I can assure you, yes, we will have a commission of inquiry and people will be held responsible based on the outcome, the results and the recommendations of that commission of inquiry' — Mark Phillips
  • Authorities described it as the worst maritime disaster in Guyana's history
  • Investigators will examine manifest discrepancies, cargo handling, adequacy of emergency response and possible maritime regulation violations — Guyana's Department of Public Information
  • About 179 people were aboard despite the manifest listing 116 passengers and 17 crew — Mark Phillips
  • Authorities planned to begin raising the wreck using specialized flotation equipment — President Mohamed Irfaan Ali, Wednesday
  • Seabed mapping technology provided with support from ExxonMobil located the ferry resting on its starboard side, allowing engineers to plan a recovery effort — Irfaan Ali
  • 'We have not ruled out rescue just yet' — Irfaan Ali, to relatives during a late-night briefing
  • The ferry's captain and mate tested positive for marijuana; maritime personnel responsible for operations placed on administrative leave — officials
