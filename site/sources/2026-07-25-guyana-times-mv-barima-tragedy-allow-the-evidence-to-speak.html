Guyanese press letter 25 July 2026

The MV Barima Tragedy: Allow the evidence to speak

Guyana Times · Haseef Yusuf (letter writer; no further description given in the piece)

Read the original at Guyana Times ↗

What this source establishes

In a letter to Guyana Times, Yusuf argues that blame is being assigned prematurely and opportunistically by the opposition, and that judgement should wait for a proper expert investigation. He rejects the inference from the vessel's age to unseaworthiness, citing hundreds of millions spent on rehabilitation from 2021 to 2024 and over $100 million budgeted this year. He answers the charge that the newer MV Ma Lisha is used for 'parties and frolicking' by pointing to wharf structural failures and delays. He characterises the tragedy as 'a potential ballot winner for the opposition'.

The author’s argument, as stated

Blame for the MV Barima sinking is being assigned prematurely and opportunistically by the opposition, and judgement should be withheld until a proper expert investigation produces evidence.

Key claims

  • The writer dates the onset of national mourning to July 18.
  • Social media commentary on causes includes 'distorted, inflammatory assumptions and conclusions', which the writer calls dangerous.
  • Critics have concluded government is to blame and are urging Ministers Edghill and Indar to resign or be fired.
  • The writer argues age does not determine seaworthiness, citing hundreds of millions of dollars spent from 2021 to 2024 and over $100 million budgeted for rehabilitation this year.
  • The writer says opposition claims that MV Ma Lisha is used for 'parties and frolicking' ignore that the wharf to berth the vessel has faced structural failures and delays, and that the vessel plies other routes.
  • The writer characterises the tragedy as 'a potential ballot winner for the opposition'.

Quotations

  • “This tragedy is now a potential ballot winner for the opposition. This smacks of insincerity and hypocrisy under the guise of mourning.”— Haseef Yusuf, letter writer
  • “Let us spare the families the additional emotional trauma of having to deal with unsubstantiated and falsely loaded claims. Let the actual evidence speak.”— Haseef Yusuf, letter writer
