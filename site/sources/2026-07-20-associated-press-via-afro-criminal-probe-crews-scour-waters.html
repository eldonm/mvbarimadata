Guyana launches criminal probe as crews scour waters for survivors after ferry capsizes

What this source establishes

NOT ARCHIVED AS A SEPARATE FILE: retrieval returned a condensed rendering only, and the same AP story is preserved verbatim-adjacent in the Jamaica Gleaner record for 20 July. Logged for the distinct figures it carried: at least 32 of 67 rescued absent from the manifest, and only 10 bodies recovered by 20 July. It describes the vessel as 80 rather than 87 years old and reports passengers seeing water seeping into cargo areas during the voyage.

Figures cited in this source

10 bodies recovered by July 20; 67 rescued of whom at least 32 were not on the manifest; documented 116 passengers and 17 crew

Key claims

  • At least 32 of 67 rescued passengers were not listed on the manifest — authorities
  • Significantly more people were aboard than the documented 116 passengers and 17 crew — Prime Minister Mark Phillips
  • 'Where negligence, misconduct or criminal wrongdoing is established, those responsible will face the full force of the law' — Mark Phillips
  • By July 20, only 10 bodies had been recovered
  • The rescued captain and at least one crew member tested positive for marijuana and remained in police custody — officials
  • Ticket clerks at state-run ferry terminals have long unofficially sold seats without adding buyers to manifests; Public Works Minister Juan Edghill characterised this as 'a criminal action'
  • President Irfaan Ali visited survivors in Mabaruma, describing the encounter as 'deeply emotional'
  • The vessel is described as 80 years old — an outlier against the 87-year figure used across the rest of the corpus
  • Multiple passengers reported water seeping into cargo areas during the voyage

Quotations

  • “a criminal action”— Juan Edghill, Public Works Minister, on unrecorded ticket sales

About this archive

An independent, citation-first record of the sinking of the MV Barima on the night of Saturday 18 July 2026, compiled from 233 published documents and checked line by line against them.
