Key claims

  • Second press record of the 29 July APA picket outside the Office of the President, published same-day.
  • Jean La Rose: "We are very sad about the lives lost in the MV Barima tragedy. We feel it is a tragedy that should not have happened."
  • La Rose: "For you to fix something, you have to know what the problem is, we have to be consulted. We want to have inclusion in fixing everything that has to be fixed."
  • "Protesters called for the resignations of Minister of Public Works Juan Edghill and Minister of Public Utilities and Aviation Deodat Indar."
  • [ALLEGATION — and the most serious unsourced assertion found in this survey] News Room states the three men "were charged with murder on Tuesday after revelations that unlisted passengers were aboard and crew members tested positive for narcotics." No toxicology report, police release, DPP statement or court document is cited for the narcotics assertion, and no other outlet in this survey states it as the basis of the charges. It is recorded here strictly as News Room's characterisation and NOT as an established fact about any of the accused.
  • "The government is conducting an international Commission of Inquiry."
  • Toll given as "76 passengers and crew members have been rescued, 73 persons have been confirmed dead, and 30 remain unaccounted for."
  • The report notes the demonstration "highlighted longstanding challenges facing Indigenous communities, including high transportation costs and safety concerns."
