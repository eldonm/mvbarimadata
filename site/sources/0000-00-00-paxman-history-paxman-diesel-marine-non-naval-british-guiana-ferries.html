Paxman Diesel Engine Applications - Marine, Non-Naval — entry for MY Pomeroon (1936), MY Lady Northcote (1937), MY Barima (1939)

What this source establishes

An engine-builder heritage register entry, unconnected to the 2026 disaster, documenting the three British Guiana Railways twin-screw ferries and their Paxman machinery: Pomeroon (1936), Lady Northcote (1937) and Barima (1939). It records the builder as Ferguson Brothers (Port Glasgow) Ltd, the order placed through the Crown Agents for the Colonies for the Transport and Harbours Department, a length of 125 ft 9 in with 29 ft beam, two 6-cylinder Paxman MRZ engines of 180/200 bhp each, and an average speed of 8.5 knots. It is the strongest independent source in this corpus for the MV Barima's provenance and dimensions.

Key claims

  • Barima one of a class of three: Pomeroon 1936, Lady Northcote 1937, Barima 1939 — delivered across four years, not as one batch
  • Builder: Ferguson Brothers (Port Glasgow) Ltd
  • Ordered by Crown Agents for the Colonies for the Transport and Harbours Department of British Guiana Railways
  • Length 125 ft 9 in, beam 29 ft
  • Two 6-cylinder Paxman MRZ engines, 180/200 bhp each at 1,000 rpm, reverse/reduction gearboxes
  • Average speed 8.5 knots, maximum nearly 10.5 knots
  • Barima's engines cost GBP 1,970 each
  • Auxiliaries: one 47 bhp 3RQ genset (16 kW), one 53 bhp 4RQ set (30 kW), both at 1,100 rpm

Quotations

  • “Twin screw vessels, built by Ferguson Brothers (Port Glasgow) Ltd. Ordered by Crown Agents for the Colonies for the Transport and Harbours Department of British Guiana Railways.”
  • “The engines for Barima cost £1,970 each.”
