Regional press wire 19 July 2026

Huge wave suspected in Guyana ferry disaster as 67 rescued

Jamaica Observer · CMC (Caribbean Media Corporation)

Read the original at Jamaica Observer ↗

What this source establishes

A CMC dispatch in which Minister Edghill denies any mechanical or overloading cause, speaking partly to the children of a missing crew member. It carries the corpus's highest stated passenger capacity for the MV Barima at 397, against the 'more than 300' cited elsewhere the same day. Edghill characterises overloading claims as deliberate misinformation and public mischief. The wave-and-tide explanation is presented as the leading hypothesis.

Figures cited in this source

116 passengers and 17 crew aboard; 67 rescued including 15 children; no deaths reported in this article

Key claims

  • Preliminary reports suggest a massive wave may have caused the MV Barima to capsize — Public Works Minister Juan Edghill
  • 'It's a big wave. That's all we hear. It's from everybody who hears it. But there will be a full investigation into what happened' — Juan Edghill, speaking to the children of crew member Richard Moe Sr
  • 'There was no engine problem or any mechanical problem... and it had nothing to do with overload or taking in water' — Juan Edghill
  • 'It had to do with the tide' — Juan Edghill
  • MV Barima licensed to carry 284 tonnes of cargo; cargo manifest showed 268 tonnes — Juan Edghill
  • MV Barima licensed to carry 397 passengers and only had 116 aboard — Juan Edghill
  • Overloading claims dismissed as 'misinformation and something to create panic and public mischief' — Juan Edghill
  • Health Minister Dr Frank Anthony coordinating a major medical response across Regions Two and Three
  • 67 people including 15 children rescued — Guyana's Department of Public Information

Quotations

  • “It's a big wave. That's all we hear. It's from everybody who hears it. But there will be a full investigation into what happened”— Juan Edghill, Public Works Minister
  • “The misinformation of overloading as the cause of this incident must be dealt with for what it is. Misinformation and something to create panic and public mischief and we deny that in the very strongest terms”— Juan Edghill, Public Works Minister
  • “So, your father…let's hope and pray that he's coming in soon”— Juan Edghill, to the children of crew member Richard Moe Sr
