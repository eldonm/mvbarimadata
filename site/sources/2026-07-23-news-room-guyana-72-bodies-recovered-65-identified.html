MV Barima Tragedy: 72 bodies recovered, 65 identified

News Room Guyana

Read the original at News Room Guyana ↗

What this source establishes

News Room Guyana's Thursday night update, five days after the sinking, recording the rise from 65 to 72 bodies recovered with 65 identified and 22 assets still deployed. The rescued figure remains at 76. It functions as a fixed point in the recovery curve, showing seven more bodies found in a day while the survivor total had stopped moving, and confirms the salvage and repositioning plan was still pending execution. It carries a photo caption showing victims of the tragedy.

Figures cited in this source

72 bodies recovered, 65 positively identified; 76 rescued

Key claims

  • Five days after the MV Barima sank, authorities had recovered 72 bodies with 65 victims positively identified.
  • Prime Minister Mark Phillips provided the update on Thursday night.
  • A total of 22 assets including 19 vessels and three aircraft remained actively engaged in the search and recovery efforts.
  • The number of rescued passengers and crew remains unchanged at 76.
  • The search area was expanded to 2,100 square kilometres.
  • The captain and an engineer remain in custody after testing positive for narcotics; another person who oversaw the loading turned himself in for questioning.
