Regional press news 25 July 2026

Guyana to launch international inquiry into MV Barima Tragedy

Caribbean Broadcasting Corporation (CBC Barbados) · Grace-Anne Smith (CMC)

Read the original at Caribbean Broadcasting Corporation (CBC Barbados) ↗

What this source establishes

CMC wire copy carried by Barbados's state broadcaster CBC reporting President Ali's announcement of an international and independent Commission of Inquiry, with the five-member panel to be named Sunday. It reproduces the announced scope and powers, and says the members were selected for independence, technical expertise and proven ability and will determine their own procedures. The wire version carries no opposition reaction, and frames the inquiry as a government initiative; the objections prominent in Guyanese coverage of the same announcement are absent.

claimed dozens of lives (no specific figure)

Key claims

  • President Dr Mohamed Irfaan Ali announced that an international and independent Commission of Inquiry will be established to investigate the capsizing of the MV Barima.
  • The five-member panel is to be announced on Sunday and will comprise experts in law, marine engineering, maritime safety and disaster investigation.
  • The members had been selected for their independence, technical expertise and proven ability, and would determine their own procedures and methods of work.
  • The President described the mandate as 'a thorough and impartial examination of the disaster, including the events leading to the vessel's capsizing and sinking, the emergency response, and all related matters.'
  • Issues to be examined include the vessel's loading, passenger boarding procedures, conditions on board, seaworthiness and maintenance history, compliance with maritime laws and safety standards, the conduct and competence of the master and crew, passenger management, life-saving arrangements, and the impact of weather and sea conditions.
  • The Commission will review the search, rescue and recovery operation.
  • The inquiry will have authority to determine whether negligence, misconduct, dereliction of duty or institutional failures contributed to the tragedy.
  • The report describes the sinking as one of the deadliest maritime disasters in Guyana's history.
  • The President had previously committed to establishing an international Commission of Inquiry once the search and recovery phase had progressed.
