Guyanese press news 22 July 2026

Opposition MP Duncan arrested for flying unlicenced drone – Walrond

Demerara Waves · Denis Chabrol (Demerara Waves editor/reporter)

Read the original at Demerara Waves ↗

What this source establishes

Demerara Waves reports that APNU parliamentarian Sherod Duncan was arrested on 21 July for allegedly flying a drone without authorisation near the MV Barima disaster area, according to Home Affairs Minister Oneidge Walrond. He was taken to Charity Police Station and released later the same day. The head of the Aviation Operators Association urged drone operators to stay clear because aircraft were flying at low altitude for the search operation. Duncan said on Facebook he was showing a small drone to Warrao children when police arrived and told the parliamentarians to leave.

Key claims

  • Duncan was arrested Tuesday (21 July) for allegedly flying a drone without permission, according to Home Affairs Minister Oneidge Walrond.
  • Walrond: 'He was flying, I understand, a drone without authorisation. Those drones cannot be flown without a proper licence and he didn't have that.'
  • Duncan was taken to the Charity Police Station and released later Tuesday; Walrond did not know whether cash bail or self-bail was required.
  • Captain Learie Barclay urged drone operators to desist from operating in the area because aircraft are flying at low altitude to assist the search, rescue and recovery operation.
  • Duncan reported on his Credible Sources Facebook page that he was interacting with Warrao children and showing them a small drone when police arrived and instructed the parliamentarians to leave.
  • If charged and convicted, his drone could be seized for a specified time and he could be fined.
  • This arrest is the reason APNU MP Dr Terrence Campbell gave for being unable to reach the wreck site during recovery operations.

Quotations
