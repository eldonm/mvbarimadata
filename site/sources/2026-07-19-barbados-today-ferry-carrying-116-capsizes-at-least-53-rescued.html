Regional press wire 19 July 2026

Ferry carrying 116 people capsizes off Guyana; at least 53 rescued

Barbados Today · Barbados Today

Read the original at Barbados Today ↗

What this source establishes

A short Barbadian rendering of the earliest AP copy, preserving the 116-aboard and 53-rescued figures that AP later revised upward. It explicitly records that officials had not yet said whether anyone had died. Its safety-equipment list matches AP's rather than Al Jazeera's. The piece serves as a snapshot of the first 24 hours, before the manifest discrepancies were reported.

Figures cited in this source

116 passengers and crew aboard; 53 rescued; no confirmed dead stated

Key claims

  • A ferry carrying 116 passengers and crew capsized with 53 rescued so far — authorities, Sunday
  • The MV Barima was sailing from Georgetown along the North Atlantic coast to Port Kaituma — Prime Minister Mark Phillips
  • A distress call was received at 11:01 p.m. local time, prompting a search and rescue operation involving state and private boats — Public Works Minister Juan Edghill, on Facebook
  • The vessel capsized near the Pomeroon River — authorities
  • The vessel was fitted with 250 life jackets, two rigid life rafts and six inflatable life rafts — Juan Edghill
  • Phillips is leading the government's response
