Guyanese press news 24 July 2026

NTC urges comprehensive, transparent and independent review into MV Barima tragedy

INews Guyana · INEWS

Read the original at INews Guyana ↗

What this source establishes

INews Guyana carries the National Toshaos Council's collective position, issued on behalf of over 250 Indigenous leaders at the close of its 2026 annual conference. The NTC describes the sinking as the single largest loss of life in Guyana's history since Independence, a characterisation no government statement in this corpus makes. It commends the President, Prime Minister and cabinet by name while demanding that no stone be left unturned in an independent review reaching beyond immediate circumstances to the regulatory frameworks and safety protocols governing maritime transport. It also sets out a long-term support agenda including psychosocial care and educational assistance for affected children.

Figures cited in this source

Described as the single largest loss of life in Guyana's history since Independence

Key claims

  • The National Toshaos Council, representing over 250 Indigenous leaders, issued a statement at the conclusion of its 2026 Annual Conference.
  • The NTC described the incident as representing the single largest loss of life in Guyana's history since Independence.
  • The NTC acknowledged the President's declaration of three days of national mourning and said dedicated prayer sessions were held during the Conference.
  • The NTC called on the Government to leave no stone unturned in conducting a comprehensive, transparent and independent review of every aspect of the incident.
  • The NTC said the review must examine the broader systems, policies, regulatory frameworks, operational procedures and safety protocols governing maritime transportation.
  • The NTC urged sustained long-term assistance including psychosocial care, financial and social support, and educational assistance for affected children.
  • The NTC commended emergency responders, medical personnel, disciplined services and volunteers, as well as the President and Prime Minister.

Quotations

  • “This heartbreaking incident, which represents the single largest loss of life in Guyana's history since Independence, has plunged our nation into mourning.”— National Toshaos Council statement
  • “calls on the Government of Guyana to leave no stone unturned in conducting a comprehensive, transparent, and independent review of every aspect of this incident”— National Toshaos Council statement
