Historical & context letter to the editor / fleet provenance correction 26 January 2012

MV Torani and MV Malali were built by Sprostons in then British Guiana

Stabroek News · Captain R E W Adams (letter to the editor)

Read the original at Stabroek News ↗

What this source establishes

In a letter to Stabroek News, Captain R E W Adams corrects the public record on the provenance of three T&HD river ferries, stating that only the Makouria was UK-built, at Ferguson's Port Glasgow yard in 1959, while the Torani and Malali were built domestically by Sprostons Ltd in British Guiana, with keels laid in 1958 and 1961. He writes that the Torani was built using the Makouria's drawings and handed to the T&HD in October 1960, and that the Malali's superstructure was altered for a live-aboard crew.

Key claims

  • MV Makouria: Ferguson Shipbuilders Yard No 428, Port Glasgow, 1959; launched 23 July 1959 and towed to Guyana
  • MV Torani: built by Sprostons Ltd, keel laid 31 July 1958 using Makouria's drawings; handed to T&HD October 1960
  • MV Malali: built by Sprostons Ltd in British Guiana, keel laid 31 May 1961, superstructure altered for a live-aboard crew
  • Establishes the Ferguson yard supplied the T&HD across two decades (Barima 1939, Makouria 1959)

Quotations

  • “Only MV Makouria of the three vessels in question was constructed in the United Kingdom.”
  • “MV Malali was not constructed in the United Kingdom, but in Guyana (BG) by Messrs Sprostons Ltd”
