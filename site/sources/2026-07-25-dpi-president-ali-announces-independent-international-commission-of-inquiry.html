Official document legal instrument 25 July 2026

President Ali announces independent international Commission of Inquiry into MV Barima tragedy

Department of Public Information

Read the original at Department of Public Information ↗

What this source establishes

The DPI release announcing the Commission of Inquiry. President Ali commits to a five-member commission of local and international experts, to be unveiled 26 July, with a scope covering loading, passenger procedures, seaworthiness, maintenance records, maritime law compliance, crew competence, lifesaving arrangements and weather. The commission is empowered to determine whether negligence, misconduct, dereliction of duty or institutional failures contributed, and to recommend legislative, regulatory and operational reform. No chair is named, no reporting deadline is set, and no legal basis or statute is cited.

Official statement recorded in this source

"The commission will be tasked with conducting a thorough, impartial examination of this tragedy, including the circumstances leading to the capsizing and sinking of the vessel, the response efforts, and all related matters."

Key claims

  • An independent international Commission of Inquiry will investigate the MV Barima tragedy.
  • The five-member commission is to be unveiled on July 26, 2026.
  • Commission to comprise local and international experts in law, marine engineering, maritime safety, and disaster investigation.
  • Scope includes vessel loading, passenger procedures, seaworthiness, maintenance records, maritime law compliance, crew competence, lifesaving arrangements, and weather conditions.
  • The inquiry will also evaluate search, rescue, and recovery operations.
  • The commission is empowered to determine whether negligence, misconduct, dereliction of duty, or institutional failures contributed to the tragedy.
  • The commission will recommend legislative, regulatory, and operational reforms to prevent recurrence.

Quotations

  • “The commission will comprise distinguished experts and professionals with extensive knowledge and experience in relevant fields, including law, marine engineering, maritime safety, and disaster investigation.”— Dr Mohamed Irfaan Ali, President of Guyana
  • “The commission will be tasked with conducting a thorough, impartial examination of this tragedy, including the circumstances leading to the capsizing and sinking of the vessel, the response efforts, and all related matters.”— Dr Mohamed Irfaan Ali, President of Guyana
  • “stand together in compassion and solidarity with the families of the victims and survivors”— Dr Mohamed Irfaan Ali, President of Guyana
