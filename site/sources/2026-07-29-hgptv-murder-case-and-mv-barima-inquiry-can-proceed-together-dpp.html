Key claims

  • "The murder prosecution arising from the MV Barima tragedy does not have to await the findings of the Commission of Inquiry, the Director of Public Prosecutions has said amid mounting criticism over the charges."
  • CRITICAL NEGATIVE FINDING: "The statement did not disclose the evidence contained in the police file or explain publicly how prosecutors intend to establish the legal elements required for murder. Those details are expected to emerge through the court proceedings." This corroborates Chabrol's observation that intent and malice went unaddressed.
  • The DPP's cited precedents are "the 2018 Berbice piracy case and the prosecutions arising from the 2020 general and regional elections."
  • [NEW] "The charges have attracted criticism from attorneys, political groups and residents of Granderson's home community of Den Amstel." — first published reference to community reaction; note this gives Granderson's home community as Den Amstel, whereas the charge sheet address is Fellowship, West Coast Demerara.
  • [NEW] "The Vigilant Political Action Committee has also warned against making the captain and crew scapegoats while possible failures by regulators, administrators and senior officials remain unexamined."
  • HGPTV qualifies the political-direction allegation: "No evidence has been publicly presented showing that the charges resulted from political direction."
  • Frames the division of labour: "The commission will examine the technical, regulatory and institutional circumstances surrounding the disaster and recommend reforms or further action. The court will determine whether the prosecution can prove murder against the three accused."
  • This article states no count of charges; HGPTV's editorial note observes it "references three murder charges (one per accused)."
