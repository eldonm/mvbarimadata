Counselling, psychosocial support extended to MV Barima survivors and bereaved families

Guyana Chronicle · Staff Reporter

What this source establishes

The Chronicle reports Human Services and Social Security Minister Vindhya Persaud said her ministry mobilised social workers, counsellors and psychologists and established a command centre at the Umana Yana, Kingston, on Sunday 19 July. Speaking on the New Day radio programme, she said social workers accompany families travelling to Region Two, teams are deployed in Regions One and Two and at Smith Creek, and support is being prepared for first responders and Transport and Harbours Department staff, with a public hotline under discussion.
