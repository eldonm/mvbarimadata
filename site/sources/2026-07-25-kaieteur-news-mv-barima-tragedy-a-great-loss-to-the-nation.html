Guyanese press letter 25 July 2026

MV Barima Tragedy: A Great Loss to the Nation

Kaieteur News · Dr Asquith Rose, writing on behalf of the Macedonia Joppa Voluntary Committee (MJVC) of East Corentyne, Berbice

Read the original at Kaieteur News ↗

What this source establishes

A condolence letter to Kaieteur News from Dr Asquith Rose on behalf of the Macedonia Joppa Voluntary Committee of East Corentyne, Berbice. It aligns the community with the President and government in mourning and stresses solidarity across Guyana, the diaspora and the Caribbean. It dwells on the dead children and on the orphans and widows left behind, framing consolation in Christian terms with a citation of Matthew 19:26. The letter uses the government's 'one Guyana' language sympathetically, urging the nation to mourn as 'one Guyanese family in one Guyana'.

The author’s argument, as stated

A community and faith-framed letter of condolence urging the nation to mourn together as 'one Guyanese family in one Guyana' and to pray especially for the dead children, orphans and widows.

Key claims

  • Dates the sinking to Saturday, 18th July, 2026, on a voyage to Port Kaituma.
  • States that candles are burning and people praying in Guyana, the Diaspora, the Caribbean and the wider world.
  • Singles out 'the children who have been denied the opportunity to grow up' and the bereaved families' orphans and widows.
  • Cites Matthew 19:26 and the 'consensus of Christian theology' that 'God will never give them more than they can bear or handle'.
  • Uses the government's 'one Guyana' framing sympathetically, in contrast to opposition and columnist usage of the same phrase as a reproach.

Quotations

  • “we stand with you in unity to lift your hearts and strengthen your hopes as one Guyanese family in one Guyana”— Dr Asquith Rose, Macedonia Joppa Voluntary Committee
  • “we must pray for the dead, especially the children who have been denied the opportunity to grow up to be young men and women”— Dr Asquith Rose, Macedonia Joppa Voluntary Committee

Actors named

  • Dr Asquith Rose, letter writer
  • Macedonia Joppa Voluntary Committee (MJVC) of East Corentyne, Berbice, community organisation
  • Dr Irfaan Ali, President of Guyana
