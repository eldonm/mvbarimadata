Regional press news 25 July 2026

Mother, three children buried as Guyana ferry tragedy death toll climbs to 73

The Nation (Barbados) / nationnews.com · Nationnews Desk

Read the original at The Nation (Barbados) / nationnews.com ↗

What this source establishes

NON-VERBATIM RETRIEVAL: condensed rendering only; see file header. The highest death toll recorded anywhere in this corpus at 73, with 69 identified and 22 funerals held. It is built around the burial of Rachel Wells and three of her children at Bush Lot, Region Five, noting her husband survived and that the family had been travelling to Region 1 to see relatives for the first time in nine years. It documents the physical toll on divers working in murky water and strong currents, some falling ill. President Ali raises the possibility of a full salvage operation, which authorities hoped to avoid because it risked disturbing remains.

Figures cited in this source

Death toll 73 after one more body recovered Friday, 69 identified; 22 funerals held; manifest listed 116 passengers and 17 crew; as many as 179 believed aboard; 76 survivors

Key claims

  • A mother and three children were buried Friday in Bush Lot, Region Five — Rachel Wells and her children Natalia, Ezekiel and Matevik
  • Wells' husband survived the tragedy; the family had been traveling to Region 1 to visit relatives for the first time in nine years
  • The 87-year-old vessel capsized after being struck by a wave while traveling from Georgetown to Port Kaituma
  • 'Deepest condolences to this family' — Education Minister Sonia Parag, attending the funeral on behalf of government leadership
  • 22 funerals have occurred and one additional body was recovered Friday, bringing the death toll to 73, of which 69 have been identified — Prime Minister Mark Phillips
  • 76 survivors were rescued and recovery operations continue as a national priority — Mark Phillips
  • Divers faced murky, muddy water and strong currents, with some divers becoming ill during operations — President Irfaan Ali
  • Authorities are attempting to move the vessel, which lies on its side; if that fails a full salvage operation may be necessary, which authorities hope to avoid due to potential disturbance of remains — Irfaan Ali

Quotations

  • “deepest condolences to this family”— Sonia Parag, Education Minister, at the funeral on behalf of government leadership
