Recovery operations continue overnight as MV Barima death toll rises to 72

What this source establishes

The Chronicle reports President Ali said work to reposition the MV Barima wreck continued overnight, and that Prime Minister Mark Phillips confirmed two additional bodies recovered during Thursday 23 July, bringing the total recovered to 72, of whom 65 had been positively identified. Phillips said 22 assets — 19 vessels and three aircraft — remained deployed across a search area of about 2,100 square kilometres extending to Waini Point, and repeated that unauthorised drones are prohibited. The article states 179 were aboard and 76 rescued.
