Guyanese press news 22 July 2026

Opposition Leader calls for Edghill's resignation

Kaieteur News · KNEWS (Kaieteur News staff)

Read the original at Kaieteur News ↗

What this source establishes

Kaieteur News reports that with 53 bodies recovered and at least 50 still missing, Opposition Leader Azruddin Mohamed demanded Public Works Minister Juan Edghill's resignation, calling the sinking negligence on the minister's part. The report records the revision from a manifest of 116 passengers and 17 crew to approximately 179 people after CCTV review. Edghill declined to address resignation calls and Prime Minister Mark Phillips dismissed them as 'noise', saying any decision rests with President Ali. Mohamed alleged political interference over cargo loading and said the captain was being unfairly blamed.

Key claims

  • As of 22 July: 53 bodies recovered and at least 50 people still missing.
  • The vessel is described as 87 years old and capsized several miles offshore on the night of July 18.
  • Mohamed spoke to GoMoseley Radio Station on Tuesday morning from the site of the capsizing.
  • Mohamed: 'The vessel was overloaded and it's a negligence of the Minister of Public Works. I'm calling on the Minister of Public Works to resign immediately.'
  • Edghill initially stated 116 passengers and 17 crew were aboard; officials later revised the figure to approximately 179 people after reviewing CCTV footage.
  • Edghill declined to address resignation calls at a Tuesday evening press conference: 'I will address any issue at the appropriate time, not tonight.'
  • Prime Minister Mark Phillips dismissed resignation calls as 'noise' and said any decision on Edghill's position rests with President Ali.
  • Mohamed alleged political interference compromised safety decisions, claimed the captain was being unfairly blamed, and that ministerial figures allegedly pressured him to load additional cargo.
  • The APA, APNU MPs and the AFC have all called for Edghill's removal or resignation.

Quotations

  • “The vessel was overloaded and it's a negligence of the Minister of Public Works. I'm calling on the Minister of Public Works to resign immediately”— Azruddin Mohamed, Leader of the Opposition
  • “I will address any issue at the appropriate time, not tonight.”— Juan Edghill, Minister of Public Works
  • “noise”— Mark Phillips, Prime Minister, on resignation calls

Actors named
