Key claims

  • "Three senior crew members of MV Barima have been arrested following an indictment of 72 counts of murder" — third independent statement of the 72 figure. The article does not explain the distribution.
  • Gives figures: "76 people have been rescued, while 73 bodies have been recovered. Out of these, 68 victims have been identified with the majority sent to their families for proper burials." — NOTE this contradicts the archive's 69 identified (24 July) and the 5-unidentified figure from iNews/Kaieteur on 28-29 July (which implies 68 identified of 73).
  • Confirms names/ages: Captain Kevin Price 40, Chief Mate Rondell Roberts 42, Goods Superintendent Delon Granderson 33; before Chief Magistrate Judy Latchman, Georgetown Magistrates' Court, Tuesday 28 July.
  • "The offenses are indictable, meaning the suspects cannot enter a plea." Case proceeds at Charity Magistrates' Court on 3 August.
  • "According to the police, the charges have been laid after receiving legal advice from the Director of Public Prosecutions following the investigation into the sinking of MV Barima."
  • [ALLEGATION — assertion by an accused person] "The captain reported that 'the 87-year-old ship had several technical problems' including missing radar and Search and Rescue Transponders, which he alleged had existed for years without repair."
  • Quotes PM Mark Phillips: "Search and recovery operations remain active, with 18 assets currently deployed in support of the operations." — a new operational figure not in the archive.
