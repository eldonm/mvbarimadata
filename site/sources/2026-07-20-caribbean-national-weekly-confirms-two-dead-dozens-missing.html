Regional press news 20 July 2026

Guyana confirms two dead, dozens missing after MV Barima ferry capsizes

Caribbean National Weekly · Jovani Davis

Read the original at Caribbean National Weekly ↗

What this source establishes

A diaspora-press account filed while the official toll was still two, setting out the announced investigation's scope and the hospital triage numbers. It is one of the earliest records of Edghill calling the manifest discrepancies criminal and tying them to revenue from unregistered passengers. It carries two survivor accounts and Eve Jerome's allegation that the captain dismissed passenger alarms about water entering the boat. It notes President Ali was returning from Canada.

Figures cited in this source

Two confirmed dead; at least 70 missing; at least 67 rescued by Sunday evening; manifest listed 116 passengers and 17 crew

Key claims

  • 'As of six o'clock... we only have two persons confirmed dead' — Public Works Minister Juan Edghill
  • At least 67 people rescued by Sunday evening; efforts to determine how many were aboard complicated by an inaccurate passenger manifest — officials
  • Several rescued passengers were not listed on the ferry's official manifest — Juan Edghill
  • President Irfaan Ali was returning to Guyana from Canada Sunday night — Prime Minister Mark Phillips
  • Two rescued crew members tested positive for cannabis during medical examinations — Mark Phillips
  • Manifest discrepancies described as 'criminal', reflecting an apparent attempt to carry unregistered passengers for additional revenue — Juan Edghill
  • 50 rescued passengers taken to Charity Hospital, four requiring additional treatment; 17 more to Lima Hospital — health officials
  • Survivor Devon Gaskin escaped by jumping into rough waters and holding a floating gas cylinder until rescue shortly after 5 a.m. Sunday
  • 'People started raising alarms that water was entering the boat, but the captain kept telling us that everything was normal' — Eve Jerome, mother of two missing children
  • CARICOM, the European Union and the United States extended condolences and support

Quotations

  • “As of six o'clock... we only have two persons confirmed dead”— Juan Edghill, Public Works Minister
  • “These are deeply concerning and shocking developments”— Mark Phillips, Prime Minister, on the positive cannabis tests
  • “People started raising alarms that water was entering the boat, but the captain kept telling us that everything was normal”— Eve Jerome, mother of two missing children
  • “Where negligence, misconduct, or criminal wrongdoing is established, those responsible will face the full force of the law”— Mark Phillips, Prime Minister
