What this source establishes

A Guyana Graphic analysis by Pamela Snow, published the day after the sinking, recording that as of 19 July 2026 the MV Barima's gross tonnage, deadweight tonnage and certified passenger and cargo capacity were not in the public domain. It gives the vessel as built 1939 and capsized 18 July 2026 near Iron Punt en route from Georgetown to Port Kaituma with 116 passengers, 17 crew and about 268 tons of cargo, with at least 67 rescued. It calls for independent technical audits of all coastal ferries, public disclosure of vessel data, digital manifest controls and an independent maritime accident investigation board.

Key claims

  • MV Barima built 1939; capsized 18 July 2026 near Iron Punt en route Georgetown to Port Kaituma
  • Carried 116 passengers, 17 crew, about 268 tons of cargo, and vehicles
  • At least 67 rescued; death toll undetermined at publication
  • Vessel Gross Tonnage, Deadweight Tonnage and certified capacity limits NOT publicly disclosed
  • Survivors described sudden severe listing, shifting cargo and rapid capsizing; local fisherfolk assisted early rescues
