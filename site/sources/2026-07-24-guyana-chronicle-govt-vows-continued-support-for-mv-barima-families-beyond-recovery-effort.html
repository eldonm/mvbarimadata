Gov’t vows continued support for MV Barima families beyond recovery effort

Guyana Chronicle · Staff Reporter

What this source establishes

The Chronicle reports President Ali told relatives at Charity, Region Two on Wednesday evening 22 July that government support would continue after search and recovery efforts end, saying “We are not going to stop… we will be here with you.” He said strong currents had prevented divers from making the progress anticipated, commended Health Minister Frank Anthony and Regional Health Officer Ranjeev Singh, and described arrangements for transporting bodies and relatives, funeral support through regional administrations, and counselling services.
