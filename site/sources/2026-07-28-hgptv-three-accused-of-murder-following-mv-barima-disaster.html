Key claims

  • Third-outlet corroboration of identities: "Price, age 40, hails from Melanie Damishana on East Coast Demerara. Roberts, 42, resides at Meadow Grove Public Road, East Bank Demerara. Granderson, 33, is from Fellowship, West Coast Demerara."
  • "The three appeared before Chief Magistrate Judy Latchman at Georgetown Magistrates' Court, where indictable murder charges were formally presented. They did not enter pleas and were remanded to custody. The case was adjourned to August 3 at Charity Magistrate's Court."
  • [NEW] On the investigation: "Investigators examined surveillance footage and cargo documentation." This is the first published indication that CCTV footage forms part of the police file — material given the PM's 24 July announcement of expanded wharf CCTV.
  • States the division of responsibility as the government has framed it: "The goods superintendent supervised wharf loading while the captain and chief mate retained authority over passenger and cargo acceptance."
  • Qualifies the toxicology: two crew members including the captain tested positive for narcotics "though test timing and impairment remain undetermined."
  • Figures: "Rescue efforts recovered 73 bodies and saved 76 people, though approximately 30 remain missing."
  • "An international five-member Commission of Inquiry will examine maintenance, seaworthiness, regulatory oversight, and institutional failures separately from criminal proceedings."
  • States no count of charges.
