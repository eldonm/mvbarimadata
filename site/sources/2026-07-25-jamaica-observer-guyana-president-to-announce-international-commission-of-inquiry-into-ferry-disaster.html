Regional press news 25 July 2026

Guyana President to announce international Commission of Inquiry into ferry disaster

Jamaica Observer · Caribbean Media Corporation (CMC)

Read the original at Jamaica Observer ↗

What this source establishes

The same CMC wire report as carried by CBC Barbados, with minor wording differences, published in Jamaica. It records President Ali's announcement that a five-member international and independent Commission of Inquiry would be named on Sunday 26 July, with members selected for independence and technical expertise and free to set their own procedures, and describes the sinking as one of the deadliest maritime disasters in Guyana's history. As with the CBC version, the wire text carries only the government's framing and no opposition reaction.

Key claims

  • Guyana's President Dr Mohamed Irfaan Ali says an international and independent commission of inquiry will be established to investigate the capsizing of the MV Barima.
  • The five-member panel is to be announced Sunday and would comprise 'distinguished experts and professionals' with experience in law, marine engineering, maritime safety and disaster investigation.
  • The members had been selected for their independence, technical expertise and proven ability, and would determine their own procedures and methods of work.
  • The commission's mandate is a 'thorough, impartial examination' of the disaster including events leading to the capsizing and sinking, the emergency response, and all related matters.
  • Areas of examination include loading, boarding procedures, conditions on board, seaworthiness, maintenance history, compliance with maritime laws and safety standards, conduct and competence of the master and crew, passenger management, life-saving arrangements, and weather and sea conditions.
  • The commission will review the search, rescue and recovery operation.
  • The inquiry will have authority to determine whether negligence, misconduct, dereliction of duty or institutional failures contributed to the tragedy.
  • The sinking is described as one of the deadliest maritime disasters in Guyana's history.

Quotations

  • “distinguished experts and professionals”— Irfaan Ali, President of Guyana, on the composition of the panel
  • “thorough, impartial examination”— Irfaan Ali, President of Guyana, on the commission's mandate
