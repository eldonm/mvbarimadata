Key claims

  • MOST IMPORTANT NEW FACT OF RECORD: the charge particulars are published for the first time — "The indictable charge alleges that on 18th July, 2026, at a point 9.3 nautical miles on a bearing of 040° off the Essequibo Coast at Devonshire Castle, the trio murdered 72 named victims." The 72 victims are NAMED in the charge. This is the first explanation in the corpus of how the number 72 is constituted.
  • Addresses are given with more precision than the archive holds: Price "of Melanie Damishana, East Coast Demerara"; Roberts "of Meadow Grove Public Road, East Bank Demerara"; Granderson "of Fellowship, West Coast Demerara." Granderson's address is new.
  • [ALLEGATION] Opposition Leader Azruddin Mohamed "alleged the vessel was operating on a single working engine at the time of the incident, and that crew were forced to work under unsafe conditions" — unproven, and it bears on the conduct of persons before the court.
  • Mohamed: "They gave the crew a basket to fetch water."
  • [SINGLE SOURCE] Mohamed "announced plans to organise peaceful demonstrations across all ten administrative regions."
  • [ALLEGATION] APNU MP Ganesh Mahipaul "alleging that workers who had previously raised safety concerns to the T&HD faced threats of transfer or job loss", and "disputing official passenger and cargo estimates and alleging severe overloading."
  • Mahipaul compares present arrangements "to past administrations where physical spot-checks and independent load checkers were regularly deployed at the goods wharf."
  • CONTRADICTION WITHIN THE ARTICLE: the lead places the protests on "Thursday morning and evening", but the fifth paragraph refers to "Wednesday's protests"; every other outlet (Demerara Waves, HGPTV, Kiskadee) places them on Wednesday 29 July.
