What this source establishes

A tertiary aggregator page compiling the MV Barima's history and the disaster timeline. It is the only source in this corpus giving a gross tonnage (284) and hull material (steel) for the vessel, both uncorroborated and unsourced; the 284 GT figure may derive from the Kalliopi N's 284-passenger capacity. It also gives 126.5 feet in length, twin Paxman-Ricardo diesel engines, a 1939 Port Glasgow launch and 86 years of North West District service, departure about 3:15 PM on 18 July, a distress call at 11:01 PM, Coast Guard arrival about 2:30 AM, and 73 recovered, 76 rescued and about 30 unaccounted for.

Key claims

  • Scottish-built steel ferry launched 1939 at Port Glasgow; served the North West District 86 years
  • Stated 284 gross tons, 126.5 feet long, twin Paxman-Ricardo diesel engines
  • Region One population given as 38,956, without road access to the capital
  • Departed Georgetown about 3:15 PM on 18 July 2026; distress call 11:01 PM; Coast Guard arrived about 2:30 AM 19 July
  • 73 recovered (69 identified), 76 rescued, about 30 unaccounted for
  • CCTV review established 179 people aboard against a manifest of 133

Quotations

  • “the passenger manifest was found to be inaccurate”
  • “vessel loading, passenger boarding procedures, seaworthiness, maintenance record and compliance with maritime laws”
  • “a large wave striking and canting the vessel”
