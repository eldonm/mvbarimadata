Ask a question

Send a question about the MV Barima disaster and it will be answered on the questions page, from the 303 documents this archive holds. If the record cannot answer it, that is published too — what the record cannot answer is itself worth knowing.

An address is only needed if you want a reply. Neither your name nor your address is published unless you ask for it.

What happens next

Every question is answered on the questions page, with the evidence named and the limits stated. The answer is written by Claude Opus 5 reading across the whole corpus, and it is checked before it is published — against the documents, and adversarially, which is how this archive has caught and corrected its own errors. Those corrections are logged on the revisions page.

Questions are published with their answers. Your name and address are not, unless you ask for them to be.

If you are reporting a correction, the most useful thing you can send is the document. This archive would rather be corrected than be consistent.

If you hold a document this archive is missing — a certificate, a log, a tender file, an inspection report — the method and gaps page lists what has been searched for and not found.

What this cannot do

This archive does not give legal advice, does not speculate about the guilt or innocence of the three men charged on 28 July, and does not take a position on who should resign. Questions on those subjects will be answered as questions about what the record shows, or answered by saying that it shows nothing.

About this archive

An independent, citation-first record of the sinking of the MV Barima on the night of Saturday 18 July 2026, compiled from 303 published documents and checked line by line against them. It is not affiliated with the Government of Guyana, the Transport & Harbours Department, any political party, or any news organisation.

Compiled Sunday 26 July 2026, last revised Tuesday 4 August 2026. The Commission of Inquiry was sworn in on 30 July 2026 at a ceremony closed to the press; its establishing instrument was still not gazetted, and figures were still moving. Read Method & gaps before citing anything here.

This site records what the sources say and who said it. Outside the questions page it does not advance an explanation of the cause, and it makes no recommendation.

Research, retrieval, cross-checking and reasoning carried out with Claude Opus 5, Anthropic’s frontier model. Every claim is traceable to a named published document; corrections are logged on the revisions page.

Copyright & corrections

Source pages carry bibliographic detail, a research summary, extracted claims and short quotations for the purpose of comment and review. Copyright in the underlying articles remains with their publishers, and every page links to the original — please read journalism at its source.
