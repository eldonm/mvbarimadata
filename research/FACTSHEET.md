## The wreck — government position moved within 24 hours
- 28 July, Phillips: "likely to remain there as is"; nobody contacted to raise it.
- 29 July, after Mohamed's offer: government acknowledges **multiple salvage
  proposals** and says **families will decide**. Phillips reportedly concedes
  international salvage companies "were prepared to raise" it.
- Demerara Waves (28 Jul) reports the government willing to leave the vessel on
  the sea floor **even if it holds useful evidence**.
- The instrument circulated to families on 25 July, which the archive records as
  a "Declaration of Agreement", is described by Chabrol as a **questionnaire**
  asking families to choose salvage or memorial. Same instrument, materially
  different characterisation — FLAG the difference, do not merge.
- Phillips has moved off "not overloaded", now referring the question to the COI,
  against MARAD's categorical 22 July finding.
