# Anomaly analysis, 29 July 2026 — raw findings

## Corrections to earlier assumptions
1. The 284 is NOT Kalliopi N contamination — it is a category error. Wikipedia capture
   (2026-07-26_wikipedia_mv-barima-wikipedia-article.md) gives 284 GT, 198 DWT, capacity
   394 passengers + 18 crew. Edghill's "licensed for 284 tonnes" = gross tonnage, a volume
   measure, not cargo capacity. Mohamed independently cites 198 deadweight exceeded by ~70
   tonnes (2026-07-29 Kaieteur + HGPTV). The archive has never connected the two 198s.
2. The Charity barricade / state-media-only claim is NOT in this corpus. Searched all 233.
   Kiskadee has 4 files, none mentions it. Drop unless sourced externally.
3. The wreck position did not shift only after Mohamed. On 28 July — same day as "I don't
   think we've contacted anybody to raise the vessel" — Demerara Waves reported Phillips
   saying international salvage companies "were prepared" to raise it. Same-day internal
   contradiction, which is stronger than a 24-hour reversal.
4. The March 2026 tender is the scheduled docking cycle (Barima last docked 2024, next due
   H2 2026). DPI 2026-03-09: docking+overhaul tendered for three vessels — Ma Lisha
   G$199.3m, Barima G$124.5m, Hercules G$124.2m. Downgrade. Live question is narrow: was
   the tender awarded, and had docking been deferred?

## The pattern
Findings 1, 2, 3 and 10 cohere. Not concealment of a known fact: at every point where the
state's account could have been made checkable against a document, the document has not
appeared — and the state has repeatedly asserted conclusions that only that document could
support. The minister cleared the vessel on overloading using what is very likely a
gross-tonnage figure. The regulator cleared it citing a load line and certification it has
not produced. The head of government has since abandoned the overloading finding and
referred it to a commission whose instrument does not exist. The one remaining physical
object that could settle the question independently of paper is the subject of a document
circulated to bereaved families asking them to agree it stay where it is — drafted while
those families were being told righting was imminent, and two days before the technical
assessment that would later justify leaving it. And the one figure showing how the manifest
failure was distributed across living and dead was volunteered on day one and refused on
day seven.

That is what an administration looks like when its early public statements outran its
records. It is also, precisely, what a cover-up looks like. The record as it stands cannot
distinguish between those two, and the honest thing is to say so.

## The document
The MV Barima's current Certificate of Survey and Load Line Certificate, with the registry
entry stating gross tonnage and deadweight. One page decides: whether 284 was a unit error
or a licensed limit; whether 120/126 is the real cargo figure; whether 268 was inside or
~70 tonnes outside deadweight; whether the "not overloaded" finding was sound; whether the
394-passenger rating exists on paper and when it rose from 150; and — because deficiencies
are noted at the back of a certificate, per Adams 2015 — whether anyone had recorded defects
on this hull before 18 July. Cheapest document on the list; the regulator has already said
publicly it relied on it.

Second: the police charge sheet with particulars. The only document that would say what the
state actually alleges happened.
